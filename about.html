Our Heritage

A Lifelong Dedication to the Photographic Craft

Photography is not merely about pressing a shutter — it is the science of light, the psychology of emotion, and the sacred preservation of memory.

The story of RPV Studio traces back to 1998. In 2013, this passion took full physical form with the establishment of RPV STUDIO in Thoraipakkam, Chennai — a state-of-the-art portrait studio fitted with high-end digital photography equipment and comprehensive infrastructure.

RPV Studio is the brainchild of T.R.K. Vijayan, who earned his formal Diploma in Photography in Chennai (ITI) in 1996 and subsequently accumulated a decade of deep professional lab experience with the renowned Konica Color Lab (2002 to 2012).

This unique foundation in color science, film chemistry, and digital sensors gives RPV Studio a distinct advantage: we understand color fidelity, skin tones, and print longevity in a way few modern photographers do.
